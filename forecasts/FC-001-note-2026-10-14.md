# FC-001 dated note (2026-10-14) -- the calibration basis the card was computed on

This note sits beside `FC-001-mufuse.json` and changes nothing in it: the card is not edited or re-hashed, and it is
scored as registered.

The card's predictions are conditional on the calibration basis that stood when it was registered. That calibration
used the measured effective sticking, 0.45 %, from which Breunlich et al. (1987, p.331) had already subtracted the
dmud, tmut, pmud and pmut fusion losses, as the total loss per cycle in the yield relation, and it left the measured
cycle rate, 145 us^-1 (p.332), out of the likelihood. With those paired values the relation gives a yield of
130.9, 1.49 sigma from the measured 113 at the card's yield uncertainty of 12, and 1.79 sigma at the +-10 the
source prints: the measurements alone did not force the correction; the source's definition of the quantities did.
`CALIBRATION.md` now fits the three summaries with the side-channel loss at the anchor fill.
